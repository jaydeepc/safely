Safely

Your passwords live on your phone.
Let's connect this browser.

Chrome keeps nothing. When you open a login page, Safely asks your phone through the Safely Key in your pocket and fills the form.

1

Install the Bluetooth helper

Chrome extensions cannot keep a Bluetooth link open by themselves, so a tiny helper does it. It only passes encrypted messages along — it cannot read them. In the project folder, run:

scripts/install-host.sh

Then come back — this page notices on its own. If macOS asks, allow Chrome to use Bluetooth.

2

Bring your key and phone close

Looking for your Safely Key…

3

Pair with your phone

On your phone open Safely → Devices → Pair a browser, then press the button.

Waiting for your phone…

Does your phone show the same code?

Confirm on the phone too.

Paired with your phone
